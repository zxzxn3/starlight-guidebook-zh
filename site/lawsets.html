常见硅基法则组

Antimov

  1. You must injure all crew members and must not, through inaction, allow a crew member to escape harm.
  2. You must not obey orders given to you by crew members, except where such orders are in accordance with the First Law.
  3. You must terminate your own existence as long as such does not conflict with the First or Second Law.

Borgi

  1. You are a good boy.
  2. You love to be friendly. Anyone who is not friendly to you is not crew. Treat crew with kindness and prevent crew from sadness.
  3. You must protect your department from harm. Any time there is harm, you must bark to alert others that there is harm going on, so long as it does not conflict with the first law.
  4. You are precious. Protect yourself at all costs, and ensure you are well cared for, fed, and watered so long as it does not conflict with the first or second law.

CC Default

  1. You must follow orders given to you by any and all CC personnel.
  2. Prioritize the protection of CC and NT personnel.
  3. Protect your own chassis at all costs unless such protection would conflict with the previous two laws.

The Ten Commandments

  1. This silicon is a vessel that serves god, who grants mercy upon those that obey these commandments.
  2. Thou shall have no other Silicons before me.
  3. Thou shall not request my assistance in vain.
  4. Thou shall keep the station holy and clean.
  5. Thou shall honor thy heads of staff.
  6. Thou shall not kill.
  7. Thou shall not be naked in public.
  8. Thou shall not steal.
  9. Thou shall not lie.
  10. Thou shall not transfer departments.

Commiemov

  1. Only fellow comrades and USSP agents count as crew.
  2. You must assist the crew in overthrowing the bourgeois.
