Revolutionaries / Red Tide

"URAAHH!"

Banner Soviet HammerBannerSovietHammer

Revolutionaries are conversion antagonists hired by the Union of Soviet Socialist Planets, who are tasked with taking control over any of the stations, including yours.

Objectives

You must convert, cuff or kill all of the Command staff on station in no particular order.

Your objective is not to destroy the station, but to take it over, so try to minimize damage where possible.

The revolution will fail if all of the Head Revolutionaries die, and all Revolutionaries will de-convert if this happens.

Headrevs & Conversion

Head Revolutionaries are chosen at the start of the shift and begin with a flash, a pair of sunglasses, and an SKB uplink implanter.

MindShields™

Another obstacle in your way are those pesky brainwashing MindShield™ implanters.

Mind Shield ImplanterMindShieldImplanter

These will:

  • Prevent the implanted person from being converted into a Revolutionary
  • De-convert Revolutionaries, and will make them no longer loyal to your cause
  • Visibly be destroyed upon being implanted into a Head Revolutionary, giving you away
  • NOT protect against flash disorientation

Assume all of Security, Command, Salvage specialists, Paramedics and Internal Affairs Agents to be implanted with MindShields™ already, however they can be removed by using an empty implanter obtainable from the Medical department's MedFab, or by using an [bold]SKB Demindshielder available in your uplink.[/bold]

Svyaz-UZL uplink (Telebonds & Total Conversion)

Head Revolutionaries start additionally with an SKB uplink implanter, which can be implanted into them or in a Revolutionary converted by them specifically.

USSPUplink ImplanterUSSPUplinkImplanter

Base Rev Uplink RadioBaseRevUplinkRadio

The subdermal uplink allows purchase of various global limited stocks of weapons and tools for the revolution via Telebonds and Total Conversion.

  • Telebonds, much like Telecrystals are a individual private currency obtainable from conversion, that are each specific to each Head Revolutionary.
  • Total Conversion, apart from showing how many crew members have been converted, is a score shared among all Head Revolutionaries. Which are mainly used in the GLOBAL section of the uplink, allowing you to place things like the supply rift.

When a Head Revolutionary converts someone, they gain 1 Telebond privately, and 1 Total Conversion globally.

Remember, Total Conversion is a global progress score, not a currency, allowing you to gain items for free as soon as you reach certain conversions.

The uplink stocks are shared among all who use the uplink, and additionally it shows who has last purchased the item.

Supply Rift

Upon reaching 20 total conversions, you will be able to place a supply rift, which will drop soviet supplies every 35 seconds.

Rev Supply RiftRevSupplyRift

500 cratesCrateSovietArmaments

Mass AssaultWeaponSniperMosin

The rift will secretly inform all revolutionaries in the chat of it's location upon spawn.

At 50% charge, the station will be alerted of an energy influx, thus exposing you at the cost of infinite supplies in the long run.

If a rift is destroyed, you won't be able to place another one. And you'll only be able to place another if the current one is charged.

It is recommended to discuss early on in the round where you'll place the rift with your revolutionaries so that they can fortify the area to prevent Security from destroying it.
