Psychologist

PsychologistToyFigurineMedicalDoctor

  1. The Psychologist report directly to the Chief Medical Officer.

  2. The Psychologist is authorized to perform a full psychometric workup on any crew member who is willing.

  3. Examinations of unwilling crewmembers may only occur with written approval by the Chief Medical Officer and one the following: HoS, Magistrate, Captain.

    1. This also applies to any medication prescribed by the Psychologist.
  1. Examinations may result in the following actions, with a sign off from the Captain or crew member's head of department:
    1. Demotion from their current position, if the patient's mental state leaves them unable to fulfill their duties.
    1. Prescriptions of Pax or other similar drugs for those who are found to be extremely violent.
    1. Increased observation of prisoners or crew who are deemed a danger.
    1. Lightening of sentences for prisoners who have shown good behavior and a change of moral character.
  1. The Psychologist may request to consult with a prisoner at any time to evaluate their mental state.
