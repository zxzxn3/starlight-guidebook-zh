Gas Manipulation

Atmos has a wide variety of tools and equipment at their disposal for the manipulation of gasses throughout the station.

Warning: unanchoring (unwrenching) most devices containing gas will leak gas into the atmosphere. You can safely unanchor canisters, portable scrubbers, condensers, and thermomachines.
