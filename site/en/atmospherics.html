Atmospherics

Atmospherics is the engineering subdepartment responsible for maintaining the station's atmosphere, and ensuring that it is safe and breathable for the crew.

Atmospherics is run by Atmospheric Technicians, who are overseen by the Chief Engineer.

Responsibilities

Generally, you'll be responding to atmospheric upsets throughout the station. These can range from minor leaks to full-on fires or spacing incidents.

People from other departments may also request canned gasses for their department duties, such as plasma for the Research Department or carbon dioxide for Chemistry.

In your downtime, you may seek to improve the station by setting up rooms with different atmospheres for Voxes and other species, or by setting up production of exotic gasses for the station's needs.

When there's no work to do, you may be expected to help regular Station Engineers with their tasks, and mentor new Station Engineers and Atmospheric Technicians.
