Malign Rifts

Malign Rifts

Cosmic Malign RiftCosmicMalignRift

Machine APEMachineAPE

As your cult progresses, Malign Rifts will begin to spawn throughout the surrounding environment. These rifts in realspace bleed unstable astral power, and should be located as soon as possible!

Malign Rifts can be expunged by an A.P.E set to Nullspace stabilizer particles or by a Holy Book.

Cultists may stealthily absorb a Malign Rift by interacting with it.

Successful absorption of a Malign Rift will permanently empower the cultist doing so, increasing the strength of all the Influences they possess, and doubling the Entropy they gain from siphoning crewmembers. Empowered cultists also no longer need to breathe, and are immune to the vacuum of space. Empowered cultists also gain the Null Fragmentation influence, which can be used to convert a Cyborg into an Entropic Colossus, or to corrupt the station AI's laws.

Empowered Malign Rifts

Malign RiftCosmicMalignRift

Empowered Malign RiftCosmicMalignEmpoweredRift

A Colossus may empower a Malign Rift, transforming it into an Empowered Malign Rift. Unlike ordinary rifts, empowered rifts are immune to the decay that normally claims malign anomalies. To do so, the Colossus must use its Ingress ability on an existing Malign Rift.

An empowered rift can hold one critical or dead non-cultist humanoid, preserving it within the rift. Each currently stored host strengthens the Colossus and its minions, allowing the rifts to serve as a source of growing malign power.
