Misc

Emergency Maintenance Access

Called by: Any two Command members.

Conditions: An emergency situation requires crew to be able to freely move through the station, such as during a loose engine or during war when Code Delta has been called.

Must be ended as soon as the situation is resolved.

Ended when: The emergency no longer requires broad maintenance or external access.

Ended by: Any Command member.

Station-Wide Emergency Access

Called by: Any two Command members.

Conditions: An emergency situation requires crew to be able to freely move through the station, such as during a loose engine or during war when Code Delta has been called.

Must be ended as soon as the situation is resolved.

Ended when: The emergency has passed.

Ended by: Any Command member.

ITG Salvage Team

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Activation time: 1 minute.

Conditions: Command needs to contract the ITG Salvage team for a specific job; common examples are assisting security with threats or conducting recovery operations.

Ended by: Any one of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

Dismiss ITG Salvage Team

Called by: Any one of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

Conditions: The contracted job is complete, or their assistance is no longer needed.

Self-Destruct Codes

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative plus any one Command member OR Captain or NanoTrasen Representative plus any three Command members, with Central Command approval.

Activation time: 30 seconds.

Conditions: A direct order from Central Command has been issued to destroy the station, or for instances when allowing the station to continue is more of a threat than it is worth.

One-time use per shift.
