Valves

Valves are a quick way to halt or permit gas flow through a pipenet.

Gas ValveGasValve

Signal Controlled ValveSignalControlledValve

Pressure Controlled ValvePressureControlledValve

Gas Passive GateGasPassiveGate

Gas Pressure RegulatorGasPressureRegulator

All valves do not require power to operate.

Valves may look simple, but they can enable a wide variety of complex pipe systems, including:

  • Signal valves responding to air alarm or logic gate outputs
  • Pneumatic valves controlling variable venting of waste gasses from a burn chamber
