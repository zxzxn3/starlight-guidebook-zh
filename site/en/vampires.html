Vampires

"You are not a monster. You are hunger."

A vampire is a stealth antagonist who starts the round as a normal crewmember, but gains access to a suite of supernatural powers. Your strength scales with your total drained blood, while abilities consume your current blood pool.

Quick Start

Use Toggle Fangs to extend your fangs. Get close to a target and bite to drink blood. Track two things: total blood drained and current fullness. At 150 total blood, you unlock class selection.

Important Limitation Each target has a cap(200u) on how much blood you can drain from them.

Base Abilities Every Vampire Has

Glare — paralyzes and silences nearby targets while dealing stamina damage over time. Great for creating a fed window.

Rejuvenate — instantly clears stuns and restores stamina; the stronger version also purges reagents and heals multiple damage types.

Power System: What Actually Matters!

Total drained blood unlocks new abilities (e.g., 150 / 250 / 400 / 600 / 800 / 1000). Current blood is your “mana” for casting. You can be 1000+ total and still be empty right now. Some high-end effects require full power -> 1000 total blood and 8 unique victims.

Class Selection

Your class defines your playstyle. You can open these pages any time:

Hemomancer — blood magic and zone control. Umbrae — darkness, mobility, ambushes. Dantalion — thralls, illusions, social control. Gargantua — melee, durability, breaking through.

Holy Weakness

Vampires are vulnerable to holy forces, but only after they have fed on at least one victim.

Holy Water:

  • Deals burn damage to vampires who have fed.
  • If you haven't drained blood from anyone yet, holy water has no effect on you.

Holy Place

  • Standing on chapel tiles deals constant burn damage to vampires.
  • Again, this only activates once you've had at least one victim.

The Chaplain

  • Chaplains with an active bible are immune to vampire abilities. But once you reach Full Power, you can bypass faith protection.
