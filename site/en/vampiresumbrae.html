Umbrae

"Darkness is not the absence of light. It is the absence of witnesses."

Umbrae is the stealth and mobility class. Your wins come from picking the right place, light level, and timing. You are strongest where others are uncomfortable: in darkness, in chaos, and at short range.

Abilities (Total Blood Threshold | Cost)

(150 | (toggle)) Cloak of Darkness Toggles whether you are currently cloaking yourself in darkness. When in darkness and toggled on, you move at increased speeds.

(200 | 20) Shadow Snare You summon a trap on the ground. When crossed it will blind the target and ensnare them Flash effects can destroy it — place it in darkness and offangle.

(200 | 20) Shadow Anchor You summon a dimensional anchor after a delay. Casting again will teleport you back to the anchor. You will fake a recall after 2 minutes

(300 | 20) Dark Passage Teleports you to a targeted turf.

(400 | 0) Extinguish Absorbs light sources around you.

(800 | 50) Shadow Boxing Your shadows beat up a target while you stay close.

(1000 | (toggle)) Eternal Darkness When toggled, you shroud the area around you in darkness and slowly lower the body temperature of people nearby.
