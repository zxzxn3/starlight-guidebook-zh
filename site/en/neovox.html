Neo-Vox

Appearance Neo VoxAppearanceNeoVox

The main reason for Cyberneticization in Vox is, unsurprisingly, to get rid of their oxygen poisoning first and foremost.

Resistances

  • Receive 20% more heat damage, 25% more shock damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 30% less cold damage and 10% less poison damage.

With the advent of advanced cybernetics and with some help from their extensive body modifications, Neo-Vox have been able to get rid of their fatal oxygen weakness, and while they still breathe nitrogen, oxygen is no longer toxic to them.

Furthermore, due to these lung modifications, nitrogen-based gaseous sedatives no longer make you tired, and you won't breathe out ammonia anymore. Things that make other nitrogen breathers sleepy will also make you sleepy too.
