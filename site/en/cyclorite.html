Cyclorite

Mob CycloriteMobCyclorite

These intelligent inhabitants of the Abyss are emotionally reserved and have a single large eye instead of a face. They are strong but slow.

Racial Features

  • Their punches deal 13 blunt and 10 structural, however they can only swing half as fast as humans.
  • They can disarm with their punches twice as likely for 50 stamina damage, but still only twice as slow.
  • They walk 15% slower than that of humans.
  • They fall into a critical state at 150 points of damage.
  • They die at 300 points of damage.
  • They breathe nitrogen.
  • They take 15% less blunt damage and 30% less slash damage than a comparable human.
  • They take twice as long to stand up from prone and double the stamina to push themselves up.
  • Due to their size, they stretch external clothing to such an extent that they can fit two suit items on their back.
  • Their eyes are less sensitive to red colors but provide better vision in the dark.

Poison and Medicine Changes

Cyclorites suffer from metabolism issues that hinder them when injected with either medicines or toxins.

  • Medicine is metabolised at half strength meaning making medicines leave the bloodstream twice as fast.

Medicine that metabolise at 0.5 units per tick will instead metabolise 1 unit per tick. Healing amounts are not changed.[italic]

  • Poisons metabolise at double strength making them last in their bloodstreams twice as long.

[italic]Poisons that metabolise at 0.5 units per tick will instead metabolise 0.25 units per tick. Poison damage or effects are not changed.[italic]

Resistance to Movement Speed Changes

Cyclorites being heavy and strong are 50% less affected by ailments and clothing that increase or decrease their speed. Cyclorites are still slowed 100% from damage.

[italic]Speed decreases such as a hardsuit that slows them down by 30% only slows them down by 15%

Speed increases such as speed boots that speed you up by 50% only speed you up by 25%
