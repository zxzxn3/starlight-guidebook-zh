Nuclear Generator

The Nuclear Generator generates power by heating a gas through fission reactions in a [Nuclear Reactor](nuclearreactor.html), which then spins a [Gas Turbine](gasturbine.html).

## Starting the Nuclear Generator
To start the Nuclear Generator, engineers must follow these steps:
- Fill the coolant loop with a gas.
- Set the Gas Turbine's Flow Rate according to the Nuclear Reactor setup.
- Insert the Fuel Rods into the empty spots in the Nuclear Reactor grid, typically by a control rod(s) and surrounded by heat exchangers and gas channels.
- Retract the Control Rods to start warming up the Nuclear Reactor.
- As the Nuclear Reactor warms up, engineers will want to keep adjusting the Gas Turbine's Stator Load to maintain optimal RPM.
- Once the Gas Turbine is making enough power for the station, the Control Rods should be inserted to the point of maintaining Nuclear Reactor temperature.

Over time, the Fuel Rods will lose radioactivity, meaning the Control Rods should be retracted further or the Fuel Rods replaced.
