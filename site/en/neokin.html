Neo-Kin

Appearance Neo KinAppearanceNeoKin

Neo-Shadekin are not a common sight, although that's not really due to any societal hangups revolving around cybernetics like plant-based species have. It's due to the fact that to become a Neo-Kin you have to give up your only hope of trying to return to your home reality. Such an operation cements their presence in our reality forever, until the day they die.

And for many, that's a very tough pill to swallow.

Resistances

  • Receive 15% more heat damage, 35% more shock damage, 35% more bloodloss damage, 30% more radiation damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 40% less cold damage and 50% less genetic damage.

Due to the operation you performed, your body is now unable to naturally heal Burns in the dark.
