Neo-Moth

Appearance Neo MothAppearanceNeoMoth

Despite their best efforts, they were not able to get rid of their fire weakness and even with their metallic body parts they still go up in flames like tinder in a tinderbox. They can handle cold a little better, though.

Resistances

  • Receive 25% more heat damage, 25% more shock damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 45% less cold damage.

Similar to their non-cybernetic brethren, they catch on fire more easily and take 250% fire damage compared to other species.

With the advent of advanced cybernetics and with some help from their extensive body modifications, Neo-Moth have been able to get rid of their inability to properly process most foods and as such can enjoy and metabolise just about any food under the sun, including their favorite snack of choice- Boots!
