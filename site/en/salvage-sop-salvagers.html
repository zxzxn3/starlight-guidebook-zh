Specialist

  • 1.\n You report to the Salvage lead. If there is none, one is to be elected by the team until one is sent by the guild.
  • 2.\n You are given leave by the ITG to expel unwanted intruders on its ATS, by force if necessary.\n\n 2.1\n Any slain or grievously injured intruders are to be revived and taken to the nearest relevant authority, usually an NT-CC Security Department, at Salvage's earliest convenience.\n\n 2.2\n Check with your Salvage Lead before taking any enforcement actions beyond self defence.

Medic

  • 1.\n You hold the same general responsibilities as a Salvager with the extension that team well being is your primary mission.
  • 2.\n You act as the second in command of the team.
  • 3.\n Recovery of ITG humanoid assets should be prioritized over recovery of physical or potential assets.
  • 4.\n It is the Medic’s responsibility to ensure the security and welfare of any intruders detained in the process of maintaining the security of ITG assets.

Lead

  • 1.\n The Salvage Lead is responsible for all salvage operator activities.
  • 2.\n Within the jurisdiction of the ITG, you are granted the highest authority.
  • 3.\n It is the responsibility of the Salvage lead to ensure their team is in compliance with local laws and regulations at all times. The Salvage Lead is entrusted with tremendous power within ITG jurisdiction and is held to the highest standards.
  • 4.\n Due to limited ITG corporate presence, Central Command is the authorised point of contact for any issues unable to be resolved locally.
  • 5.\n The spare Salvage Lead ID is to remain secure at all times, either on the Salvage Lead or stored in the armory wall safe.
