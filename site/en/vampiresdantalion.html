Dantalion

"Power is in blood. Authority is in other people's decisions."

Dantalion is the social control class. You don't need to be the strongest fighter — you make sure nobody gets to fight back. Your resources are thralls and attention control.

Abilities (Total Blood Threshold | Cost)

(150 | 150) Enthrall: Long channel (about 15 seconds) on a humanoid. You use a large portion of your power to sway those loyal to none to be loyal to you only

(250 | 30) Pacify: Pacify a target temporarily, making them unable to cause harm.

(250 | 30) Subspace Swap: Switch positions with a target.

(400 | 30) Decoy: Leave behind a fragile duplicate that punishes attackers while you slip into invisibility. Use it to drop focus in corridors.

(600 | 100) Rally Thralls: Removes all incapacitating effects from your nearby thralls.

(800 | Toggle) Blood Bond: Tethers you to nearby thralls and redistributes damage.

(1000 | 70) Mass Hysteria: Casts a powerful illusion to make everyone nearby perceive others to looks like random animals after briefly blinding them.
