Neo-Diona

Appearance Neo DionaAppearanceNeoDiona

While a Neo-Diona is a rare sight to behold, it's not impossible. Transhumanism exists even among plant-like races, although the practice is typically shunned.

Resistances

  • Receive 65% more heat damage and 30% more shock damage.
  • Receive 25% less slash damage, 20% less blunt damage, and 35% less cold damage.

In addition, while you may take more heat damage than your non-cybernetic brethren, it actually takes more Heat Damage to light you aflame than them. Furthermore, your cybernetics, while still weak to heat, at the very least, will reduce the max fire stacks from this effect down to 3.

While rooted, you're also 5% slower than base Diona. Guess the metal adds enough weight...

WARNING! Splitting into nymphs as a Neo-Diona will turn you back into a normal Diona! Choose carefully, is it really worth it to lose your cybernetics?
