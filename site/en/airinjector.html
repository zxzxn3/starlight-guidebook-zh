Air Injector

#Air Injector The air injector is a special vent that forces gasses into the atmosphere it's exposed to.

Gas Outlet InjectorGasOutletInjector

It is primarily used to force gasses into high-pressure rooms like the station's gas storage rooms or a burn chamber.

The air injector does not require power to function.

The air injector will inject gasses into the atmosphere it's exposed to until the atmosphere reaches GasOutletInjector.GasOutletInjector.MaxPressure kPa.

The air injector's speed is proportional to the amount of gas in the injector.
