Neocyte

Appearance NeocyteAppearanceNeocyte

Warning! This species is not recommended for new players due to major gameplay changes!

Neocytes are not technically a species, but rather a broad term for a person that has modified themselves extensively with cybernetic enhancements that they can no longer be considered their own species anymore.

Despite "Cyborg" already existing as a term, it was coined for machines that had organic brains, and as such a distinction needed to be made.

Racial Features

  • Receive more heat damage, more shock damage, more blunt damage and less cold damage, depending on your subspecies.
  • Variable resistances depending on your cybernetic frame. (Access your species specific loadout from the Appearance tab)
  • Healing sources are 50% less effective on you, but you can benefit from both chemical and mechanical repairs.
  • You can heal poison damage and genetic damage with Uranium Glass and Reinforced Uranium Glass.
  • EMPs are dangerous to your cybernetics! Don't let yourself get hit, or you'll be immobilised for quite a while.
  • Subspeces selections - You can choose between being an artifically made neocyte with no original species, or Neo-(Species). They inherit their original species traits and have modified resistances, combining them together into something new.
  • You can modify your Neocyte frames and lights in Markings.
