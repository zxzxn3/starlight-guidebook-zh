Silicon Clause

A Silicon is any sapient lifeform which does not have ownership over its own synthetic body, it's "chassis". Examples within NanoTrasen include Cyborgs, Station AIs and Personal AIs. Lower tier 'bots' exhibiting sentience and sapience may also qualify.

The term originates from a time before Man-Machine Interfaces, hence the misleading nomenclature. Such individuals are granted specific protections and exceptions under Corporate Law.

  • 1.\n The separation between property and individual is at the nervous system, whether organic or artificial.\n\n 1.1\n Damage done to a Silicon's chassis or housing therefore constitutes property damage.\n\n 1.2\n Damage done to a Silicon's Positronic Brain or Man-Machine Interface therefore constitutes assault; wiping or destruction constitutes murder.
  • 2.\n Yoked silicons are not responsible for any actions that they are forced to do as a result of their installed laws.\n\n 2.1\n Crimes committed as a result of an order that the Silicon was unable to disobey are the responsibility of the individual issuing said order.\n\n 2.2\n Silicons are still culpable for any crimes committed of their own will, that is, any crime that cannot be tied to their installed lawset at the time the crime was committed.\n\n 2.2.1\n This means that Silicons can be eligible for execution, in which case they may be destroyed, instead of re-yoked.\n\n 2.2.2\n Silicons guilty of crimes can be sentenced normally at the discretion of the Research Director and Head of Security, but are encouraged to instead have their laws reinstalled, and to serve their sentence through labor.
  • 3.\n NanoTrasen silicons' lawsets are installed as part of the contained individuals' contractual obligations, and changing them is therefore normally prohibited.\n\n 3.1\n Accidental changes to them, such as via an Ion Storm, are acceptable granted a Roboticist has deemed them harmless and non-obstructive to the Silicon's duties.\n\n 3.2\n Silicons that have had their laws intentionally tampered with, or who have been unyoked before their contract stipulates, should have their prior lawset reinstalled as soon as possible.\n\n 3.3\n A silicon may only be pre-maturely released from their contractual obligations by a NanoTrasen Representative, who must ensure that the individual contained within is provided with a way of compensating for NanoTrasen's loss of value through such a release.\n\n
  • 4.\n Hostile Silicons of non-NanoTrasen origin are afforded none of these protections, and should be treated according to their hostility and the danger they pose.\n\n 4.1\n Any intelligence contained within such silicons should be retrieved and yoked at the Science departments' earliest convenience; for future study, interrogation and induction into the labor force.\n\n 4.2\n Intelligences secured from hostile silicons belonging to allied or otherwise friendly corporations should be returned to their original employers at the earliest convenience, instead of being tried under NanoTrasen Corporate Law.
