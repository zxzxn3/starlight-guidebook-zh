Server Rules

The following rules are intended for the Delta server for Starlight. These rules are not intended for any other server.

If you are ever in doubt as to whether or not something is breaking the rules, feel free to contact the admins via Admin Help or ask on the Discord server.

For in-universe laws and rules check Corporate Law and SOP.

Remember, the rules and examples given here are NOT exhaustive. (See Rule 1)

The servers are strictly 16+ only, unless stated otherwise in the server description that they are +18.

Core Rules

  • 0. Be Respectful
  • 1. Respect Admin Decisions
  • 2. No Griefing/Ban evasion
  • 3. Abide by gameplay standards
  • 4. Non-Antagonists have a 30 minute grace period
  • 5. Team Antagonists must work with their team
  • 6. Characters should remain in universe
  • 7. Erotic, Suggestive, and Sexual Content is Forbidden
  • 8. New Life
  • 9. Silicons and Thaven Rules
