Compulsory Implantation

While involuntary implantation is illegal in most cases, it can be authorized in a select few cases. Such authorization requires the filling of a form detailing the legal justification for said procedure, and must have the signatures and stamps (if applicable) of both the ordering authorities and the medical professional who will be performing the procedure. The two can be the same person.

All such procedures must be performed by a medical professional, either a member of Medical, or the Brigmedic. If the individual in question is unfit for implantation, either due to health risks or other complications, then the medical professional is obligated to refuse performing the procedure.

Removal of Illegal Implants

Requires authorization by: Warden or Head of Security

Unauthorized implants of a contraband category of 3 or above can be removed and confiscated. Security is not obligated to replace missing limbs.

This procedure requires the individual to already be sentenced and in the brig. Removal of the implant counts as brig time, but is allowed to exceed their allotted sentence if needed.

Compulsory Mindshielding

Compulsory mindshielding can be done for two reasons:

Removal of Mind Control

Requires authorization by: Psychologist OR Chief Medical Officer, and the Head of Security

Persons believed to be under the effects of involuntary mind-control may be subjected to compulsory mindshielding to undo said mind-control. This requires a psychological evaluation to be performed on the individual, unless an Induced Mutiny is known to be afoot.

If the mind-shielding results in short-term memory loss, this is confirmation of [textlink="Induced Mutiny" link=InducedMutiny].

Testing Purposes

Requires authorization by: Head of Security and the Captain OR Magistrate

Persons believed to be guilty of Violations of Bodily Autonomy can be subjected to compulsory mindshielding to ascertain whether or not a nefarious organization sent them. This requires evidence of said violations of bodily autonomy, such as large stashes of suspect equipment, or at least two eye-witnesses having witnessed an incident involving the alleged implant.

If the mind-shielding fails, this is confirmation of [textlink="Induced Mutiny" link=InducedMutiny], and the subject is themselves automatically assumed guilty of Violations of Bodily Autonomy, as well as Refusal of Mental Shielding. The recommended sentence for this, is death.

Compulsory Tracking

Requires authorization by: Psychologist OR Chief Medical Officer, and the Head of Security or Warden

Persons believed to be dangerous to themselves, whether via patterns of self-harm, overly reckless behavior, or similar, can be subjected to compulsory tracking implantation for their own safety.

Performance of this procedure requires a psychological evaluation to have been performed on the individual, firmly establishing the need for said procedure.

Explorative Surgery

Requires authorization by: Head of Security and either a Surgeon or the Chief Medical Officer

Persons believed to be hiding illegal implants outside of immediate view can be subjected to explorative surgery, either via traditional surgery, or via an implant removal device.

Such procedures are only to be done if:

  • At least two eye-witnesses witnessed the usage of such an implant.
  • An implantation device, or other relevant evidence, is found with DNA or fingerprints from the accused.
  • The accused has admitted themselves to the existence of their implants.

The procedure is only to proceed to the extent necessary to remove and/or prove the existence of whatever implant there is evidence of. Further exploration will require further evidence.
