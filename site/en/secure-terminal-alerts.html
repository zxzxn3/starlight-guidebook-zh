Alert Levels

Code Gamma

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members, with Central Command approval.

Conditions: An existential threat poses a risk of losing the station that security cannot handle on Red Alert, such as a revolution or a war.

Please be sure to submit with clear reasoning in your request.

Ended when: The existential threat has been resolved or sufficiently contained. Code Gamma must be active for at least 10 minutes before it may be ended.

Ended by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Code Violet

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, Chief Medical Officer, Research Director, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Conditions: A confirmed biological or viral outbreak poses a station-wide threat and warrants elevated containment measures across all departments.

Ended when: The outbreak has been fully contained and the threat to the crew has passed. Code Violet must be active for at least 10 minutes before it may be ended.

Ended by: Any one of: Captain, Head of Security, Chief Medical Officer, Research Director, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

Code Psi

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, Research Director, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Conditions: An overwhelming hostile silicon threat is present on the station.

Ended when: The threat has been neutralized. Code Psi must be active for at least 10 minutes before it may be ended.

Ended by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, Research Director, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.
