Neocyte Frames

Neocyte FramesClothingOuterArmorNeocyteMediumBasic

The foundation of a Neocyte is their cybernetic frame, the thing that truly makes them distinct from their purely-biological brethren. Currently, there are 6 Neocyte frames approved for usage by NanoTrasen and Central Command. They have been split up into Light, Medium, and Heavy.

Light Frames

Basic-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteLightBasic

The Basic-Class light frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 5% less slash damage, 5% less pierce damage, and 5% explosion resistance.

Hermes-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteLightSpeed

The Hermes-Class light frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 15% more slash damage and 15% more pierce damage.
  • Gain a +5% sprint modifier and a +5% walk modifier.

Medium Frames

Basic-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteMediumBasic

The Basic-Class medium frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 10% less slash damage, 5% less blunt damage, and 5% less pierce damage.
  • Gain a -5% sprint modifier.

Hecate-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteMediumMagic

The Hecate-Class medium frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 15% less poison damage, 15% less radiation damage, and 15% less genetic damage.
  • Gain a small amount of biological resistance and some light cold insulation, in exchange for heating up more easily.
  • Gain a -10% sprint modifier.
  • Give off a mild purple glow.

Heavy Frames

Basic-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteHeavyBasic

The Basic-Class heavy frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 25% less slash damage, 10% less pierce damage, and15% less blunt damage, and 25% explosion resistance.
  • Gain a -25% sprint modifier and a -15% walk modifier.

Nyx-ClassClothingOuterArmorNeocyteHeavySpace

The Nyx-Class heavy frame boasts the following specs.

  • Receive 10% less heat damage, 15% less cold damage, and 25% explosion resistance.
  • Gain solid heat insulation, and moderate cold insulation.
  • Gain a -15% sprint modifier and a -10% walk modifier.
  • Gain the ability to move through open space without a space suit, provided you have something to cover your head and your own source of air.
