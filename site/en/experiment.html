Experiment

Guide ExpieGuideExpie

Experiments are simple, unremarkable Sawians, often called cannon fodder. They're officially described as a mutated mix of a human, fox and rat.

While being a mix of multiple species, they are mostly canine so certain foods, like coffee, chocolate, alcoholics and garlic/onions will poison them. They can, however, eat raw meat without issue. Their claws make them adept at unarmed combat, and their eyes emit a permanent bioluminescent glow, allowing them to see in the dark.

Experiments are rather cowardly and anxious in nature, fitting more in worker roles rather than leading. Some would call them mischievous.
