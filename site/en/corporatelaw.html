Corporate Law

On Space Station 14, stations operate under abbreviated Corporate Law. All crew, passengers, and visitors aboard the station are expected to follow these laws, and in turn are protected by them.

Domestic threats, anyone rightfully hired by Central Command and/or NanoTrasen are usually protected by Corporate Law.

Humanoids afflicted by DNA-altering parasitic or viral infections should be arrested when possible.

Enemies of the Corporation, including hostile boarders, are not protected by Corporate Law, and are only required to be treated according to Prisoner Treatment rules when resources allow it. They are not entitled to revival if killed. If an Enemy of the Corporation escapes confinement they are to be eliminated and transported to Central Command in a body bag at the end of shift.
