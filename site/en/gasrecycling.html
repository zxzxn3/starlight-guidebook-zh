Gas Recycling

Gas recycler is a machine that converts waste gasses into breathable ones.

Gas RecyclerGasRecycler

Specificaly it can recycle Carbon Dioxide and Nitrous Oxide into Oxygen and Nitrogen.

The machine has a two pipenet connections, for gas input and output.

For recycler to work, tha gas needs to be pressurized to 3000 kPa heated to 573,15K, and the pressure at the inlet needs to be higher than outlet. If these conditions are not met, the gases will just pass through without getting recycled (as will unrecyclable gases).

Gas recycler circuit boardGasRecyclerMachineCircuitboard

The machine can be constructed using a circuit board, which can be made at a circuit imprinter. It first needs to be researched by science though.

Gas recycling can be used as an alternative to gas miners, or as a backup to build a redundancy in the atmospheric system. Just don't forget to cool the gas before releasing it into the station!
