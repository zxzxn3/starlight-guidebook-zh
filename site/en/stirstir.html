Stir Stir

Who is Stir Stir?

Stir Stir is the more troublesome cousin of the slightly less disreputable Bar Monkey, Pun Pun. Often called a cell stuffer, due to how frequently he finds himself behind bars. Despite everything, the charges never seem to stick for long.

Stir Stir can almost always be found locked up in Genpop in Security at the start of every shift.

Roleplaying As and With Stir Stir

Stir Stir might be behind bars, but he is not an Antagonist. Leeway may be given by Staff for breakout attempts, but murder and constant griefing is not allowed. You require 2 hours of Security Officer play time to play as Stir Stir for a reason, so you know how annoying it would be if you acted poorly.

The circumstances of Stir Stir's incarceration are intentionally ambiguous, and are left up to the Roleplayers to decide every round. Depending on what is decided as Stir Stir's crime, Security can decide to allow parole by involving the Magistrate or Lawyers in the process for procedure and Roleplay.

Antagonists and Stir Stir

Stir Stir is designed as a Roleplay-first role for players, but like all Smart Monkeys and Kobolds, you can roll Antagonist Roles as him. Your objectives will be difficult as you start behind bars.

His imprisonment may also be to his benefit. Stir Stir has a spotty past and there are plenty of reasons for the Syndicate to want him, dead or alive. Perhaps breaking out isn't always the best idea.
