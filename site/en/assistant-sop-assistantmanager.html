Assistant Manager

Assistant ManagerToyFigurineAssistant

  1. Assistant Managers report directly to the Captain, and answer to all Command members.

  2. The Assistant Manager is responsible for coordinating Assistants and delegating tasks out to them.

  3. Assistant Managers are to coordinate with the station's departments to ensure efficient assignment of Assistants to where they are needed.

  4. Assistant Managers must not modify the IDs of non-Assistants.

  5. Assistant Managers are NOT part of Command, and their authority is limited to managing Assistants only.
