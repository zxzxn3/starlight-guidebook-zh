Portable Scrubber

The portable scrubber is a portable machine that can be used to scrub the atmosphere in a room.

Portable ScrubberPortableScrubber

It is invaluable for quickly removing unwanted gasses from a room when regular scrubbers are working too slow, or when a scrubber is not present.

The portable scrubber requires power through a nearby LV cable to function.

The portable scrubber automatically starts removing all non-breathable gasses from the room when it is bolted (wrenched) to the floor.

It will stop scrubbing when unbolted (unwrenched) from the floor or when its internal volume is full. The scrubber has an internal capacity of PortableScrubber.PortableScrubber.Volume liters.

Dumping Waste and Storage

The scrubber's internal volume can be emptied by anchoring (wrenching) onto a connector.

Stations commonly have a dedicated emptying point to quickly transfer the waste from the scrubber to the station's wastenet.
