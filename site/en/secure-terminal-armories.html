Armories & Pods

Gamma Armory

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 1 minute.

Conditions: Code Gamma is active. Standard armament is insufficient for security to respond to the active threat. The armory provides additional lethal equipment for security personnel.

Must not be called during a War Declaration.

One-time use per shift.

Recalled when: The armory is no longer needed, or its position is blocking the evacuation shuttle dock. Two minutes must elapse before recall is available.

Recalled by: Any Command member.

Psi Armory

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, Research Director, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 1 minute.

Conditions: Code Psi is active. Standard armament is insufficient for security to respond to the active hostile silicon threat.

Must not be called during a War Declaration.

One-time use per shift.

Recalled when: The armory is no longer needed, or its position is blocking the evacuation shuttle dock. Two minutes must elapse before recall is available.

Recalled by: Any Command member.

Emergency Medical Pod

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Chief Medical Officer, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 2 minutes.

Conditions: Medical is damaged and can no longer treat crew, or is overwhelmed and needs more room to treat crew.

One-time use per shift.

Recalled when: The medical emergency has passed and the pod is no longer needed, or its position is blocking the evacuation shuttle dock. Two minutes must elapse before recall is available.

Recalled by: Any Command member.

Unlock Escape Pods

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Unlocks in: 90 seconds.

Conditions: A critical station emergency requires immediate crew evacuation, and the emergency shuttle is unavailable, delayed, or compromised.

One-time use per shift.
