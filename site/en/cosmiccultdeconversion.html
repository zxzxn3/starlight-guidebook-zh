Deconversion is but one method for dealing with an encroaching cult. Crew aboard the station may alternatively wish to invest in Mindshield implanters.

These state-of-the-art devices can prevent outside influences from brainwashing crewmembers, providing a powerful utility against the cult's influence.

The Chaplain's attuning renders them immune to nearly all of the cosmic cult's powers.
