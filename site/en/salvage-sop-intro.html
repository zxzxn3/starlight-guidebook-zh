Salvage

These procedures apply to all salvage members, unless otherwise stated in their specific SOP

§1. Salvage's Purpose

  • 1.\n Salvage’s first and foremost objective is profit and reputation for the Guild.
  • 2.\n Secondarily, their lodging-arrangement with the ITG stipulates that they ensure the continued safety and profitability of their host-ATS.

§2. Uniform Requirements

  • 1.\n Clothing associated with Enemies of aligned Corporations may only be used under permission of the Salvage Lead.\n\n 1.1\n This permission does not extend to NT affiliated stations.\n\n

§3. Expeditions

  • 1.\n Any contraband and weaponry found is to be stored in the ATS armoury. The Salvage Leader may approve items for use.
  • 2.\n Salvage material should be stored for trade with the local station.
  • 3.\n Any damage to the ship/crew must be reported and repaired.
  • 4.\n Highly dangerous expeditions may only be undertaken with the express permission of the Salvage Lead. Going on unauthorized expeditions is grounds for demotion.

§4. Weapons

  • 1.\n While on the ATS or other ITG missions, non-standard weapons must be authorized by the salvage lead but are otherwise not restricted.\n\n 1.1\n This does not apply for non ITG installations.\n\n

§5. Hiring/Firing

  • 1.\n Central Command has a contract with the ITG to allow the temporary loan of employees to become part of the salvage team that is on the automated trade station. These employees are to give up their position at the end of the shift.
  • 2.\n Contractors may be hired/fired at the discretion of the Salvage Lead.\n\n 2.1\n Additional contractors must also be approved by the relevant authority over said contractor.\n\n 2.2\n The Salvage Lead may be removed by Central Command under the authority of the ITG. In extreme cases, the Salvage Team may vote for the removal of the lead. Appropriate paperwork should be filed in the event of a vote.\n\n

§6. End of Shift

  • 1.\n At the end of shift, the ITG has agreements in place to use Central Command as a central point for transport around space. As such, all ITG contractors are permitted and required to take the CentComm shuttle at the end of shift.

§7. Use of Force

  • 1.\n While on the ATS or performing sanctioned ITG activities such as local space salvaging or expeditions, Contractors may act in the defense of themselves and ITG assets.\n\n 1.1\n During expeditions to hostile locations, local hostiles are to be considered enemy combatants. Use of lethal force is encouraged and treatment of wounded not required.
  • 2.\n While off ITG installations, salvagers are visitors. Team members should not provide any assistance to the station outside of contractual obligations
  • 3.\n While on the ATS or performing sanctioned ITG activities such as local salvaging, expeditions or activated contracts, equipment is un-restricted given permission from the Salvage Lead.\n\n 3.1\n Contractors are expected to follow Corporate Law and regulations when aboard local stations as visitors.\n\n 3.2\n The Salvage lead is expected to take into consideration the risk and requests of local security when utilizing enemy equipment when contracted to a local station.

§8. States of Emergency

  • 1.\n When local space is in a state of emergency (Red or above) ITG employees are to regularly check on the security of the ATS.
  • 2.\n Local security forces may contract for assistance in matters they cannot handle. Payment is to be secured before stepping off the ATS, though this may be overlooked if the threat posed threatens the safety of the ATS as well and verbal permission from station leadership is acquired, or CC has authorized operations.
  • 3.\n When under contract by local security forces, additional resources such as communication and access cards may be released to the extent that the ITG-NT shared force agreement allows.\n\n 3.1\n During these contract periods, salvage is recognized by NT stations as members of security for the purposes of Corporate Law, but do not fall under the SOP of NT. \n\n 3.2\n If the station is under Martial law, then any orders from the contract holder is to be considered law so long as said orders do not negatively impact assets of the ITG. \n\n 3.3\n The Salvage Lead reports to the Captain of the station, but may cancel the contract at any time if they feel that the venture is not profitable for ITG. \n\n 3.4\n When under contract, Contractors are expected to maintain professionality and cohesion to show ITG’s competency in the market.

§9. Interaction with locals

  • 1.\n Local stations are often in agreements to lend engineering and medical resources if they exceed local ITG capacity, but the stations may charge for these services.
  • 2.\n Other resources such as administrative work, special equipment from science, or restricted weapons from security may be purchased using bartered recovered assets through the station cargo department.
  • 3.\n If pricing agreements cannot be reached or other issues arise between ITG Contractors and NanoTrasen Stations, Central Command is a recognized third party arbitrator for any disputes.
