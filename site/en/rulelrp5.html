Rule 5

You are a team antagonist if the game tells you you're a team antagonist in your character menu. You may not intentionally harm, hinder, or go against your fellow team antagonists, you have a goal that's much easier to complete with your team. You should listen to your team leader.

This does not mean that separate teams of antagonists must work together. (i.e. 2 separate teams of nuclear operatives.)

Examples of team antagonists include:

  • Nuclear Operatives
  • Zombies
  • Dragon (Space Carp)
  • Revolutionaries
  • Terror Spiders
