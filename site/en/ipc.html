Warning! This species is not recommended for new players due to major gameplay changes!

Integrated Positronic Chassis, abbreviated as IPC

Racial Features

  • Receive 50% more shock damage, 20% more blunt damage and 10% less slash/caustic damage. You are immune to genetic damage and all toxin damage except for radiation.
  • You burn for less time than organic lifeforms, due to your composition.
  • Deal 6 blunt damage with your mighty fists of steel!
  • You don't need to eat or drink, but you do need to stay charged. You can take power directly from APCs or sit in a borg recharger- both work.
  • You do not manage your internal temperature the same way that organic life forms do, instead relying on internal fans to heat or cool your body. Your internal fans will suffice in standard station environments without issue.
  • You're very resistant to spacing, as temperature doesn't affect you normally, you are immune to pressure damage, and you don't need to breathe. Long-term existence in vacuums or areas of extreme temperature, hot or cold, will require usage of a cooling unit to regulate your internal temperature.
  • Play music with your in-built MIDI synthesiser!
  • Chemicals don't work on you, and your "doctor" is a roboticist. Welding heals physical injuries, HV cables fix electrical damage, glass fixes your eyes, plasma fixes radiation, and Oil packs restore your "blood".
  • EMPs are lethal to you! Don't let yourself get hit, or you'll run out of power and require manual rebooting.
  • Internal Headset! It has two slots. Choose wisely!

Helpful tip - You need to unlock your panel and then use a screwdriver to get access to your internals, this includes the comm keys! Once open, right click yourself and go to "Eject" - You'll then pull out the last key you put in.
