Permit Examples

Engineering Goggles - From Engineering: If you got the goggles from Engineering, you only need the Chief Engineer's stamp. - Purchased from ITG: If you bought them from ITG, you need both the Salvage Lead's stamp and the Chief Engineer's stamp.
Mk 58 - From Security: If you got the Mk 58 from Security, you only need either the Warden's stamp or the Gunnery Sergeant's stamp. - Purchased from ITG: If you bought it from ITG, you need both the Salvage Lead's stamp and the Warden's stamp or the Gunnery Sergeant's stamp.
Medical Jaws of Life - No Origin (e.g., found in maintenance): Since it has no departmental origin and is approved for Medical and Security, you only need a stamp from any one Relevant Leader: the Chief Medical Officer, Warden, Gunnery Sergeant, or Head of Security.
Green Scrapper Beret - As an item not approved for any NT-CC department, you only need the Salvage Lead's stamp.
Cat Ears - As a Tier 1 Rival Corporation item, you only need either the Warden's stamp or the Gunnery Sergeant's stamp.
CODE-4 "Triage" - As a Tier 4 Affiliated Corporation weapon, you need stamps from MedTak, the Head of Security, and the Captain.
Blood-Red Hardsuit This entry is restricted to crew with Secure Knowledge. - As Tier 4 Rival Corporation combat gear, you need stamps from the Head of Security, the Captain, and Central Command.
