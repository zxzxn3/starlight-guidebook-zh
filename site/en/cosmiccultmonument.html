The Monument

Before you set about your goals, The Monument must be Beckoned by your cult leader. When first built, the Monument is invisible, intangible, and effectively imperceptible to any non-cultist. Once empowered, the Monument will become both visible and tangible to all individuals aboard the station.

It is for this reason that the Monument is best hidden from prying eyes, and in a relatively defendable location.

Entropy may be infused into the Monument to empower it. As you infuse the Monument with entropy, the cult will collectively gain access to more Influences and Glyphs, which can be attained by interacting with the Monument.

Cultists can interact with the Monument to purchase additional Influences. Entropy stored in the monument for the sake of purchasing is per person, so one cultist purchasing an ability will not spend any entropy collected by other cultists.

Beckoning the Unknown

Plushie Cosmic CultPlushieCosmicCult

Should you succeed in fully empowering the Monument to the third stage, it will hunger for one last threshold of entropy - fuel to feed the final curtain call. Once enough entropy has been infused, the empowered Monument will glow, signalling that it can now be used to Beckon the Unknown. Any cultist may do so by interacting with the Monument.

Once the Monument is ready to activate, cultists must activate it in order to regain access the Monument's store and glyphs - the end cannot be forestalled.

Be prepared to defend the Monument during the Beckoning, as just one waylaid action from a single disruptive soul will bring this ritual to a sudden, unceremonious halt.

Glyphs

Glyphs may be Scribed by interacting with the Monument. You may also Unscribe an unused Glyph in a similar manner at The Monument.

Be aware that activating a Glyph will sap the health of all cultists aiding in the Glyph's activation!

  • Glyph of Knowledge allows a non-cultist brought upon it to be converted into a fellow cosmic cultist! Chaplains and individuals with a mindshield cannot be converted using this glyph.
  • Glyph of Projection will allow the cultist using it to temporarily escape their body as a free-floating and imperceptible Astral Form, allowing the cultist to spy on unsuspecting crewmembers.
  • Glyph of Warding transmutes any spacesuit - hard or soft - into the cosmic cult's special Entropic Armor.
  • Glyph of Blades randomly transmutes any sharp object into one of the cosmic cult's Unique Weapons.
  • Glyph of Cessation transmutes a portable atmospherics scrubber into an unanchored Vacuous Spire.
  • Glyph of Truth allows any non-cultist brought upon it to be converted into a fellow cosmic cultist, even if they are a chaplain or protected by a mindshield.
