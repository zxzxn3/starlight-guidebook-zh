Terror Spiders

"Spiders! Oh, no! I am arachnophobic! "

Mob Terror PrinceMobTerrorPrince

Terror spiders are capture antagonists that kill all humanoids that they see.

Mob Terror PrincessMobTerrorPrincess

Initial spider (Princess of terror)

You are princess TIER 3 spider.

Abilities

You can open powered doors, spin a web and breach welded vents.

Extending your population

You have Lay the egg action which allows you to spawn TIER 1 eggs with spiders. eggs will hatch into spiderlings after a 90 seconds and then spiderlings will grow into TIER 1 spiders with type which you selected before after another 90 seconds.

TIER 1 spiders

Mob Terror RedMobTerrorRed

Terror red

It's combat spider. It has Strong web, bigger health and damage.

Also can open powered doors.

Can evolve into TIER 2 purple terror after spinning 36 webs.

Mob Terror GreenMobTerrorGreen

Terror green

It's support spider. It can lay eggs into humanoids, so with time eggs will hatch into spiderlings inside humanoid's body.

Also has Slime web which slows humanoids speeds to 30% of their original speed(instead of default 50%).

Can evolve into TIER 2 white terror after injecting 4 eggs into humanoids.

Mob Terror GrayMobTerrorGray

Terror gray

It's a stealth spider. It has Invisibility ability which makes it invisible at any web.

Also has Stealth web which more harder to see.

Can evolve into TIER 2 black terror after spinning 36 webs.

TIER 2 spiders

Terror purple

Mob Terror PurpleMobTerrorPurple

It's an advanced combat spider. In contrast to the red terror it has EMP scream ability which can drain energy from energy devices.

Can evolve into TIER 3 prince of terror after spinning 36 webs.

Terror white

Mob Terror WhiteMobTerrorWhite

It's an advanced support spider. In contrast to the green terror it has acid spit long-distance attack.

Can evolve into TIER 3 princess of terror after injecting 6 eggs into humanoids.

Terror black

Mob Terror BlackMobTerrorBlack

It's an advanced stealth spider. In contrast to the gray terror it has Jump ability which allows it to jump, jumping also knocks off humanoids.

Also has Poison web which injects toxin to humanoids standing in it.

Can evolve into TIER 3 mother of terror after spinning 36 webs.

TIER 3 spiders

Prince of terror

Mob Terror PrinceMobTerrorPrince

It's an even more advanced combat spider. It inherits all the properties of terror purple, but has more health and deals more damage.

Mother of terror

Mob Terror MotherMobTerrorMother

It's an even more advanced support spider. It inherits all the properties of terror black, and can spawn royal jelly, which heals fellow spiders.

Also has Poison web which injects toxin to humanoids standing in it.
