Hemomancer

"Blood is not evidence - blood is a tool..."

Hemomancer is the zone control and blood magic class. You shine when you: prepare the arena, manage distance, force fights on your terms.

Role in the Round

(Trap hunter): cut off routes with barriers and force bad pathing. Clinical predator: you don't always need to finish the job — sometimes a clean bite, reset, repeat is best.

Abilities (Total Blood Threshold | Cost)

(150 | 30) Vampiric Claws You channel blood magics to forge deadly vampiric claws that leech blood and strike rapidly.

(250 | 25) Blood Tendrils After a short delay, hits a 3×3 area. Heavy slow + poison — perfect for a safe bite or a clean escape.

(250 | 40) Blood Barrier Places multiple blood barriers, only you can pass through them. Use them to create “pockets” and cutoffs to buy time.

(400 | 30) Sanguine Pool Briefly turns you into a blood puddle. Great for escaping, changing angles, and slipping past obstacles.

(600 | 20) Predator Sense Helps track prey/targets. Especially useful once the station is alert.

(800 | 100) Blood Eruption Causes nearby blood to erupt, severely damaging anyone standing on it. A fightswinger: set up blood beforehand or force bleeding.

(1000 | (toggle) ) Blood Bringers Rite Ultimate aura that makes nearby people bleed and rejuvenates you. Requires full power (high total blood and enough unique victims), otherwise it won't activate.
