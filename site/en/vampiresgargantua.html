Gargantua

"When subtlety ends — you begin..."

Gargantua is the breakthrough and brawler class. You become a direct threat, but you pay for it in visibility. Your job is to choose the moment when stealth stops being profitable and end the scene with force.

Role in the Round

Breakpoint: break security formations, force entry, delete solo patrols. Evac disruption: hit critical points (bridge/med/armory) with good timing.

Toolkit (Total Blood Threshold | Cost)

(150 | 30) Blood Swell Temporarily reduces incoming damage and shortens stun durations. Limitation: you cannot use guns while it is active. After 400 total blood, it also adds extra melee damage.

(250 | 30) Blood Rush A short speed spike: chase, escape, or crash into a formation.

(250 | 30) Seismic Stomp Slam the ground: knocks down and throws nearby creatures; can break floor tiles. Great for creating chaos and resetting distance.

(600 | 0) Overwhelming Force (toggle) Automatically pries unpowered doors. While active, you can't be pushed or pulled. Cost is blood per door (~5).

(800 | 20) Demonic Grasp A ranged “hand” up to 15 tiles: immobilizes the target and can pull them to you in combat mode.

(1000 | 30) Charge A charge to break through and finish targets.
