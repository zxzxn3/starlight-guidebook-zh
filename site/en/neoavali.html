Neo-Avali

Appearance Neo AvaliAppearanceNeoAvali

Being a Neocyte comes easy to Avali, as they already live in a culture of extensive cybernetic modifcation. The prime example being the Nexus which is a Augmented Reality (AR) implant every individual recives in their society.

Resistances

  • Receive 40% more heat damage, 25% more shock damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 50% less cold damage.

With the advent of advanced cybernetics and with some help from their extensive body modifications, Neo-Avali have been able to get rid of their inability to properly process most foods and as such can enjoy and metabolise just about any food under the sun.

Oxygen/Water-Based medical chemicals remain a lethal danger for them, however.
