Lagomorph

Mob LagomorphMobLagomorph

An artificially-uplifted species of anthropomorphic mammals that have a clear resemblance with Sol's Lagomorpha. Corporate interests saw their uplifting upon first contact as a necessary solution to a labor shortage.

  • Receive 25% more heat damage and 25% less cold damage and 10% less blunt damage.
  • Metabolic processes are 33% faster.
  • Can regenerate stamina faster and gain stamina resistance for 20 seconds, in exchange for increased hunger and thirst drain while active. This ability has a cooldown of 30 seconds between uses.
  • Deal 5 slashing damage with claws.
  • Can hear people speak and whisper from 2 tiles further.

Language

  • Lagomorphs have a pidgin language of Sol Common adapted for usage in their laryngeal vocal structure, adapting to certain characters and sounds they make. This is known as Laguine.
  • Alongside Laguine, this pidgin language is used as a gateway for many to learn Galactic Common as a second language, through intense vocal training.

Age and Names

  • Their naming structure in wider galactic society due to their origins of being an uplifted laborer species is similar to standard human onomastics.
  • Through genetic manipulation by corporate interests and wider galactic quasi-governments, their lifespan is comparable to the average human.
