Electric Sheep

Mob EeepMobEeep

Electric sheep, or Eeeps, are spacefaring creatures sometimes farmed for the power they naturally produce.

Eeeplets are the adolescent form of the Eeep, little creatures with a love for feeding on electrical energy, easily draining any power source.

They are more annoying than harmful unless allowed to consume enough power to grow up; if so, better hope you have insuls...

Mob EeepletMobEeeplet

  • They can 'pry' open any door by overcharging it.
  • Eeeplets can be examined to see their internal charge; they will transform when it reaches 100%, alerting the entire station to their presence.
  • Eeeps have a powerful passive regen but can be fully killed by butchering.
  • Adult Eeeps have an electric aura that harms on touch and are able to shoot tiny Tesla-like projectiles which have embed damage even if the target is immune to shock.
  • As an Electric sheep avoid looses. The station can't make yummy power with no engine!
