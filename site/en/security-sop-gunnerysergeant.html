§2 Leader of Security's Policing Wing

  • 1.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for organizing and coordinating the regular policing duties of Security, including but not limited to:\n\n 1.1\n Patrolling - Ensuring adequate Security presence across the station.\n\n 1.2\n Investigation - Ensuring that reported crimes are followed up on in a timely manner.\n\n 1.3\n Enforcement - Ensuring that criminals are dealt with swiftly and in accordance with SOP and Corporate Law.\n
  • 2.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for organizing, equipping and leading squads to combat hostiles on the station.\n
  • 3.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for the education and allocation of cadets.\n
  • 4.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for taking over Security in the absence of its Head.\n
  • 5.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is cleared for sentencing on par with a Security Officer.

§3 Security Quartermaster

  • 1.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for ensuring that security officers are equipped in a manner befitting the threat level.\n\n 1.1\n To this end, the Gunnery Sergeant is cleared to file and approve permits for the issuing of armory equipment on Code Blue.\n\n 1.2\n The Gunnery Sergeant may hand out armory equipment freely on Code Red.\n
  • 2.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for ensuring that the armory is accessible when mandated by alert SOP.\n
  • 3.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for ensuring that equipment loaned during raised alerts is eventually returned to the armory.\n
  • 4.\n The Gunnery Sergeant is responsible for the proper storage of contraband seized by Security Officers, via use of their armory access.
