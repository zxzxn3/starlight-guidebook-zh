ERT Teams

ERT Security

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members, with Central Command approval.

Arrival time: 5 minutes.

Conditions: Active, confirmed hostile threats have overwhelmed station security's ability to respond. Remaining security staff are insufficient to contain or eliminate the threat, and the situation poses ongoing danger to the crew.

Must not be called during a War Declaration; the Nuclear Response Team should be called instead.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: The threat has been neutralized or sufficiently contained that security can manage without external support.

Rescinded by: Any one of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

ERT Engineering

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Chief Engineer, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 5 minutes.

Conditions: Catastrophic station damage (such as structural breaches, atmospheric failure, or total power loss) cannot be addressed by available engineering staff within a reasonable timeframe, and the damage poses a direct risk to crew survival.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: Engineering staff have regained the ability to manage repairs, or the damage no longer poses an immediate threat.

Rescinded by: Any one of: Captain, Chief Engineer, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

ERT Medical

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Chief Medical Officer, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 5 minutes.

Conditions: A mass casualty event has exceeded the capacity of medical staff to treat, and crew survival is at risk as a direct result.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: The medical emergency has stabilized and staff can manage remaining casualties without external support.

Rescinded by: Any one of: Captain, Chief Medical Officer, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

ERT Janitorial

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Personnel, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 5 minutes.

Conditions: The entire station is so messy that janitorial staff cannot clean it in a reasonable time.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: The station mess has been reduced to a level that janitorial staff can handle in a reasonable time.

Rescinded by: Any one of: Captain, Head of Personnel, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

ERT Chaplain

Called by: Captain and NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 5 minutes.

Conditions: Cult activity on the station cannot be handled by existing security and the chaplain.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: Cult activity has been reduced to a level that existing security and the chaplain can handle.

Rescinded by: Any one of: Captain or NanoTrasen Representative OR any two Command members.

ERT CBURN

Called by: Any three Command members.

Arrival time: 15 minutes.

Conditions: Code Violet is active. A biological or viral outbreak has been confirmed, and containment has failed or is at imminent risk of failure. Standard medical and security response are insufficient.

Must not be called during a War Declaration.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: The outbreak has been contained and CBURN intervention is no longer necessary.

Rescinded by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR Head of Security and any one Command member OR any three Command members.

Nuclear Response Team

Called by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, or NanoTrasen Representative OR any three Command members.

Arrival time: 50 minutes.

Conditions: A War Declaration has been made against the station.

Only available after war has been declared.

One-time use per shift.

Rescinded when: The war threat has been repelled or the situation no longer requires additional security personnel.

Rescinded by: Any two of: Captain, Head of Security, NanoTrasen Representative and one Command member OR Any one of: Captain, Head of Security, NanoTrasen Representative and three Command members.
