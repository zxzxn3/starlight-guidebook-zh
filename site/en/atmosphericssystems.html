Atmospherics Systems

Atmos has a wide variety of devices catered to moving, managing, and otherwise manipulating gas. These devices often work together to maintain a safe, breathable atmosphere for the crew, or to achieve a common task.

This section delves into the various systems that Atmos has at their disposal, how they interconnect, and how they are ideally used.
