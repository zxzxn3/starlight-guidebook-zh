Neo-Resomi

Appearance Neo ResomiAppearanceNeoResomi

Parts of the romantic movement within resomi society have embraced full cybernetic augmentation like some of their Avali forbearers did before them. Although even within their own movement this is seen as the more extreme and rather opulent kind of veneration. Other resomi outside of the movement of course view this practice as highly vain. The Nexus is the only exception to this.

Resistances

  • Receive 25% more heat damage, 25% more shock damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 45% less cold damage.

Oxygen/Water-Based medical chemicals remain a lethal danger for them.
