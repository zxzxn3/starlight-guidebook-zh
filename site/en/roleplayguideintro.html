How To Roleplay

Starlight is a server that strives for a strong roleplay driven environment. While it can be somewhat subjective to define what is 'good roleplay', as different styles and tones lend to different behavior, these guides aim to provide basic guidance and advice for roleplaying in general.

This guide is by no means all encompassing and is likely to be expanded upon in the future with additional chapters and more firm examples of what we consider to be Low Roleplay behavior compared to Medium Roleplay behavior.
