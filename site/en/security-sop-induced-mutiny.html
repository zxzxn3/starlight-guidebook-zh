Induced Mutiny

Induced Mutiny is any mutiny induced by mind-altering or mind-controlling technologies, usually administered by one or more hostile infiltrators.

Previously experienced examples of these are:

  • USSP mind-control technologies, used to instigate revolution.
  • Arcane mind-control, employed by some cult organizations
  • Mind-controlling implants

The presence of these infiltrators, or their victims, can in some cases be confirmed via implantation of a NanoTrasen-brand Mindshield™.

  • Should the implantation be rejected by the body and fail, this is indicative of a hostile infiltrator.
  • Should the implanted crewmember suddenly forget what happened prior to the implantation, this is indicative of a mind-control victim.

In cases where mindshielding produces no results, despite clear signs of mind-control, the following may be attempted:

  • Consultation with the station's Chaplain, to determine arcane or spiritual influences.
  • Consultation with a Medical Professional, to determine the presence of a mind-altering implant or substance.

An Induced Mutiny can furthermore be assumed to be in effect if the enemy infiltrator tips their hand, for example by:

  • Deploying a teleportation beacon or rift, to supply weapons to the mutineers.
  • Equipping the mutineers with clear displays of allegiance, such as uniforms or arcane sigils.

If an Induced Mutiny is confirmed to be underfoot, either via a mindshielding or via open declaration, then Security is legally allowed to demand the mindshielding of any crewmember, for their own safety. Refusal of these demands is a Capital Offense under Corporate Law.

Any crewmembers upon who the mindshielding fails is still guilty of Refusal of Mindshielding, whether they actually refused or not, and should be executed.

As long as a mind-shield is able to restore the victims' original state, they should not be prosecuted for actions committed while under said mind-control, including any refusal of mindshielding.
