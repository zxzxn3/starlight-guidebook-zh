Writing

Using a pen, you can write on paper or in books. Nanotrasen provides everyone with a spare pen in their PDA.

Formatting

You can use the following sigils:

  • \[/color] — to change the color of the text
  • \[/head] — where n is the heading level (1 – 3)
  • [bullet/] — to insert a bullet point (useful for lists)
  • [bold][/bold], [italic][/italic] — for emphasis

You can combine bold and italic tags, or use the shortcut [bolditalic].

Color supports either hexadecimal or most HTML color names. For example:

  • \[/color]
  • \[/color]
