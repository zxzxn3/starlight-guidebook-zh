True Neocyte

Appearance NeocyteAppearanceNeocyte

What's a "True Neocyte", you ask? At what point of replaced limbs and organs do you no longer consider yourself to be the same as when you started? Whatever your original species actually was, you've long cast it away, and embraced your current self as your own successor.

Search for a hope and a future. Find your own answer.

Resistances

  • Receive 20% more heat damage, 25% more shock damage, and 10% more blunt damage.
  • Receive 50% less cold damage.
