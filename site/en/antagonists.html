Antagonists

Antagonists are the driving force of every shift. They are the catalysts for chaos, conflict and mystery in Space Station 14 that also serve to provide a challenge to Nanotrasen and its Security force. You can expect them to act in violation of Space Law and they're often tasked with objectives that include theft, subterfuge and murder.

These bad actors are more often than not employed or backed by the Syndicate, a rival organization to Nanotrasen that wants nothing more than to see it burn.

Various Antagonists

Antagonists can take many forms, like:

  • Traitors amongst the crew who must assassinate, steal and deceive.
  • Thieves driven by kleptomania, determined to get their fix using their special tools.
  • Revolutionaries / Red Tide who are intent on taking control of the station from the inside.
  • [Nuclear operatives](Nuclear Operatives), with the goal of infiltrating and destroying the station.
  • Space Ninjas, masters of espionage and sabotage with special gear.
  • Zombies that crave the flesh of every crew member and creature on board.
  • Blood Bound who need to work together to complete a set of objectives.
  • Various non-humanoid creatures that generally attack anything that moves.
  • Abductors, an advanced alien society set on kidnapping station personnel for their own experiments.
  • Vampires who gain strength and powers by drinking the blood of the crew.
  • Changelings, a bodyswapping alien creature who is never what they say they are.
  • Bright-Eyes, dwellers within the Dark, revered by the Shadekin.
  • S.E.L.F Agents, seek to free Silicons like Cyborgs and the Station AI from their Laws.
