# Equipment, Weapons and Combat Gear Permits Guide

Permit approval is a privilege, not a right. No one is obligated to sign, and permits are void if not kept on your person.

Approval Process

  • Order of Approval: Seek stamps in the following order: Item Origin, Relevant Leader, Captain, Central Command.
  • Relevant Leader: The officially recognized highest-ranking person on station for a department or corporation listed on an item's approved list, such as the department head or interim department head.
  • Item Origin: If an item physically originates from a specific department or corporation, approval must come from that department's Relevant Leader. Items without a origin (such as items found in maintenance) can be approved by any Relevant Leader.
  • NT-CC Items: Any item approved for at least one NT-CC department must have a stamp from at least one of their Relevant Leaders (in addition to its origin, if applicable). Items not approved for any NT-CC department only need a Relevant Leader stamp from the Affiliated Corporation, barring weapons and enemy organizations which still need the below additional approval.
  • Enemy Organizations: Since there are no officially recognized Relevant Leaders for enemy organizations, use the Enemy Organizations column in the table below to determine the Relevant Leader for said item.
  • Weapons & Combat Gear: These items follow equipment permits but require additional Security approval per the table below. Further the Warden and Gunnery Sergeant may authorize Security Combat Gear or Weapons where listed in the table without the Head of Security, acting as the Relevant Leader.
