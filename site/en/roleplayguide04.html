Acting With Mechanics

In a game like Space Station, we’re somewhat confined with what we can convey, and how clearly, without using emotes to describe actions. Using emotes is the most clear and potent way to do this, but also the slowest and it is not ideal for all situations. Some actions should be quick. But there are a handful of things you can do to act through mechanics in this game, to smooth these rough edges over.

You can control what direction your character is facing by either clicking in a direction, or entering Combat Mode and aiming with your mouse. They both have different advantages, clicking can let you snap to a facing faster, looking like you just 180’ed on your heels. Using Combat Mode however lets you pick what direction you are facing while moving, letting you shuffle sideways or walk backwards away from something.

You can also use pre-set emotes with shorthand in chat, such as “LOL” making your character laugh. There are other audio based emotes built into the emote wheel which is by default bound to the Y key. There are guides online for making custom binds as well, allowing you to set up for example, the M key emoting “scratches his beard.”

The emotes wheel also features Cloud Emotes that take the form of bubbles over your character with an icon in them that expresses something. These can be used as quick stand-ins instead of emoting to describe an expression.
