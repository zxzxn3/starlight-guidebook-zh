##Parole

Prisoners are permitted during their sentence to request parole. Warden or Head of Security may approve parole instead of incarceration at their discretion. See Parole in Security SOP for clarification and procedure.

Members of Law Department may order parole instead of incarceration at the conclusion of a trial or investigation. See Legal SOP for clarification.
