Sawian

Guide ExpieGuideExpie

Sawians are a group of artificially created species based off of various earth genomes, often manning Nanotrasen stations.
Sawians are biologically genderless, though some still sport masculine/feminine features visually.
They are not accustomed to wearing clothes. The less they're wearing the faster they move,
gradually going from 125% of base speed while wearing no clothes (Head, Outer clothing, Mask, Feet, Inner clothing, Gloves) to 95% speed with every slot filled.

#Health
Sawians are remarkably durable, made to live in conditions deadly to most species
- Most are given brain implants allowing them an advanced readout of their bodies condition.
- Their durability allows them to endure the effects of being in critical condition for some time.
At 100 damage they enter "soft critical" state, where they can crawl, talk, and use items. 
They will however take Asphyxiation Damage,
eventually passing out at 150 damage before ultimately dying at 200 damage.
- Their hastened metabolism makes them process chems and poisons faster.
- They get hungry and thirsty 3 times faster than humans, but they have an additional healing while they are not starving,
effectively making them heal twice as fast passively while not critical.
- While they are starving they gradually take 0.5 Caustic damage every second until death.

#Conformity
- Any deviation in personality or genetic composition within a species is a rarity - differences are likely from personal trinkets, scars, or accessories.
- Prior to their transfer to CC, All Sawians were given a four-digit ID number and one nickname. (e.g., #4466 John, #7201 Jimmy, #1793 Jane)
CC-NT has modified its systems allowing continued use of this system, as to facilitate a simpler transition into CC-NT employment.

NT expects Sawians to sanitize their provided ID numbers. extremely low numbers or innuendos (such as #6969, #6767, #0420, #8008) are to be replaced and Never used on station.
