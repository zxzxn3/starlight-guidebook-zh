化学品

化学品是很有力的工具，进入人体后可以产生各种效果。有些能从植物中取得，有些可以向货运部购买，还有些需要用其他化学品合成。

了解不同化学品及其效果，对处理伤害和危险十分重要。

## 药物

acrileni Acrileni

A weak natural medicine able to treat light peircing damage.

Physical description: spiny

Advanced Mutation Toxin AdvancedMutationToxin

A non-scientific absurdity that mutates people into slimes. Ever wanted to lose all your limbs?

Physical description: soothing

aloxadone Aloxadone

A cryogenics chemical. Used to treat severe burns and frostbite via regeneration of the affected tissue. Works regardless of the patient being alive or dead.

Physical description: soothing

Recipes
  • cryoxadone 2u + aloe 1u + leporazine 2u → aloxadone 4u

ambuzol Ambuzol

A highly engineered substance able to regress the progress of rotting.

Physical description: crisp

Recipes
  • dylovene 1u + ammonia 1u + mortucussus 2u → ambuzol 4u

ambuzol plus AmbuzolPlus

Further engineered with omnizine, it substantially regresses rot progress in corpses.

Physical description: crisp

Recipes
  • ambuzol 5u + omnizine 5u → ambuzol plus 10u

amoxla Amoxla

Ammonia-based chem that treats airloss and bloodloss in Avali, Resomi, and Vox, and acts like a somewhat strong poison in other species.

Physical description: opaque

Recipes
  • ammonia 1u + carbon 1u + iron 1u → amoxla 2u

arcryox Arcryox

A sickeningly blue cryogenics chemical that is able to heal extreme wounds even on the dead. It has trouble stabilizing patients however.

Physical description: icy blue

Recipes
  • lithium 1u + tricordrazine 1u + cryoxadone 1u → arcryox 3u

arithrazine Arithrazine

A mildly unstable medication used for the most extreme case of radiation poisoning. Exerts minor stress on the body.

Physical description: cloudy

Recipes
  • hyronalin 1u + hydrogen 1u → arithrazine 2u

barozine Barozine

A potent chemical that prevents pressure damage. Causes extreme stress on the body.

Physical description: viscous

barozine plus BarozinePlus

An advanced form of Barozine only found in Space Medipens. Heals less, but an overdose is less painful.

Physical description: viscous

bicaridine Bicaridine
